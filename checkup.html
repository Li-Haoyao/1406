Annual Eye Checkup Package

A comprehensive checkup package designed to support healthy vision, detect problems early, and provide practical guidance for long-term eye care.

What's included

  • Vision Assessment: examination of vision clarity, depth perception, and colour vision
  • Eye-Health Screening: checks for early signs of eye diseases such as cataracts and glaucoma
  • Retinal Imaging: advanced imaging to analyse retinal health
  • Prescription Update: review of glasses or contact lens prescriptions
  • Lifestyle & Visual-Care Discussion: advice on screen habits, lighting, and eye-care routines
Book Your Checkup

Early detection matters

Routine eye exams help identify issues such as glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration before symptoms become more serious.

Helpful for all life stages

Children, adults, and older patients can all benefit from regular eye examinations. Different ages have different needs, and routine care supports better long-term results.

Care tailored to your lifestyle

Each patient receives advice based on work, screen time, daily habits, and general eye-health needs, helping support comfort and good routines.
